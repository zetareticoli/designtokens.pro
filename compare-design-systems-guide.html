Design Tokens Pro vs Design Tokens Mastery

Design Tokens Mastery is the self-paced video course from The Design System Guide by Romina Kavčič. It is one of the most direct alternatives to Design Tokens Pro: both teach naming, documentation, and how tokens scale. They do it in different media, at different prices, and with a different relationship to Figma.

What each one is

Design Tokens Mastery is a hands-on video course. Lessons walk through what tokens are, how to document them, how to name them, then a large block on Figma Variables and an even larger one on Tokens Studio — including multi-brand themes, mathematical relationships between tokens, Airtable as a source of truth, and GitHub sync. You get PDF presentations, editable Figma playgrounds, JSON files, checklists, a certificate, and lifetime updates. The listed price is $229 (often discounted; 14-day satisfaction guarantee).

Design Tokens Pro is a written digital guide: eight chapters, 100+ pages, online access and a printable PDF, for $39. Reading the whole thing takes about a couple of hours. It focuses on taxonomy, naming, architecture, compound tokens, themes, lifecycle, and documentation, with JSON examples that are not tied to Figma or Tokens Studio.

Where Design Tokens Mastery is stronger

Choose it when the job is to operate the tools: modes in Figma, multi-dimensional themes in Tokens Studio, syncing JSON to a repo, keeping an inventory in Airtable. The course is built as click-along instruction, with playground files and checklists. If you learn better from video and your team already lives in Figma plus Tokens Studio, that is the right shape of product.

It also keeps adding lessons based on student questions — useful if you want a living video library rather than a finite book.

Where the guide is stronger

Design Tokens Pro is a reference you reopen when a naming debate starts, not a sequence of videos to finish. Token name elements, three-tier architecture, compound tokens, and documentation need clear structure and a PDF you can annotate. You do not need Tokens Studio Pro, a Figma seat, or Airtable to use it.

That matters when tokens have to survive beyond one plugin: code-only repositories, other design tools, or a team that has not standardized on Tokens Studio. The value is the mental model — how to name, layer, theme, and retire tokens — at $39 with a 30-day refund.

They are not mutually exclusive

A common split: Design Tokens Pro to decide the taxonomy and layers, Design Tokens Mastery to implement that model in Figma Variables and Tokens Studio. If you can only pick one, pick based on format — written architecture vs hands-on Figma workflow.

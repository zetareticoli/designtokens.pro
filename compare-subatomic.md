# Design Tokens Pro vs Subatomic

[Subatomic: The Complete Guide To Design Tokens](https://designtokenscourse.com/) is the video course by Brad Frost and Ian Frost. It is the closest paid educational product to Design Tokens Pro — and a different kind of product.

## What each one is

**Subatomic** is a video course (13+ hours) with Figma and code sample architecture, naming and governance workflows, PDF slides, a private Slack channel, a certificate, and optional live team training. An individual license is $900. Two seats are $1,500; teams get 20% off per seat. It is aimed at organizations juggling many products, platforms, brands, and modes.

**Design Tokens Pro** is a written digital guide: eight chapters, 100+ pages, online access and a printable PDF, for $39. Reading the whole thing takes about a couple of hours. It focuses on taxonomy, naming, architecture, compound tokens, themes, lifecycle, and documentation, with JSON examples that are not tied to one vendor.

| | Design Tokens Pro | Subatomic |
|---|---|---|
| Format | Written guide (online + PDF) | 13+ hours of video + files |
| Time to complete | About two hours | A full course, over days or weeks |
| Price (individual) | $39 | $900 |
| Tooling walkthrough | Tool-agnostic JSON and snippets | Hands-on Figma Variables, Style Dictionary, sync |
| Best when | You need a durable reference for naming and architecture | Your org wants a shared, facilitated curriculum |

## Where Subatomic is stronger

Choose Subatomic when the job is to train a team in a proven Figma + code architecture, with hours of recorded explanation, Slack, and a certificate. If you learn better from video, or procurement needs a course, it fits that budget and format.

## Where the guide is stronger

Design Tokens Pro is a reference you can reopen. Naming, token name elements, three-tier architecture, compound tokens, and documentation need clear structure and a PDF you can annotate — not 13 hours of playback. It stays useful when your stack is not Figma-plus-Style-Dictionary. At $39 with a 30-day refund, it does not require a training budget. Lifetime access includes future updates.

## They are not mutually exclusive

Use Subatomic to watch how an enterprise architecture is built; use Design Tokens Pro as the short written map of taxonomy and process. If you can only pick one, pick based on format and scale.
